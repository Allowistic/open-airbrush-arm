# Contributing

Thanks for helping build the Open Airbrush Arm.

- **Open an issue first** for anything bigger than a typo, using the bug or feature/hardware template.
- **Code** (`firmware/`, `software/`, `mocap/`) is Apache-2.0. **Hardware** (`hardware/`) is CERN-OHL-S-2.0. By contributing, you agree your work is licensed the same way.
- **Hardware changes:** include the source CAD (STEP or native), not just STL, and update `hardware/bom/BOM.csv` with part numbers and links.
- **Don't copy upstream files** from SO-ARM100 into this repo. Reference the submodule instead.
- **Safety:** note any change that affects air pressure, the valve, or the arm's motion limits in your PR description.
- Keep PRs small and focused, and describe how you tested.
